# The order: what is left to close the cycle, in sequence

This is a list, written so we do not lose it. The last three posts
built up a picture of the machine as a heat cycle: reservoirs that
remember, a working fluid that moves, a cold leg where the reading
becomes definite, and a return that carries what the solver says back
into the loop. Tonight two things are running against pinned bars,
and what we do next depends on how they land. So here is the picture
as of this evening, and then the order.

## Where the cycle stands

**Reservoirs.** Two organs remember across breaths: the notebook, a
shelf the reading writes into and reads back per slot, and the
garage, the phasor bus where committed deposits wait. Both are
durable. Only one is indispensable: severed, the notebook costs four
points of wild and sixteen of synthetic, while the garage costs about
one point of wild and nothing on synthetic. Durable and load-bearing
are different properties, and a bus that carries at half its band
can still be re-derived by the loop each breath.

**Working fluid.** The state turns over by half every breath and
never freezes. There is no ratchet. That was the finding that turned
"the air is stuck" into "the air is turbulent."

**Cold leg.** The sealed channel, the pressure cooker's road. It
exists, it carries, and it is not yet the regime the machine is
deployed in.

**Return roads.** Two are needed. The slot-to-token gate is built and
failed its bar by a hair this afternoon, buying robustness instead of
aim. The solver's refusal re-entering the loop, the release valve, has
no code at all.

**Mass.** A severance ladder found seven of seventeen million
parameters remove for nothing and that the two organs worth the most
per parameter are among the smallest. The balanced generation, three
arms that prune the dead organs and regrow the reservoir and the
grounding, is training as this is written.

## Tonight

Two units, one behind the other, on the single GPU.

The balanced generation: prune only, then prune plus a second
notebook lane and a third grounding station, then the same with a
cooker that makes the new organs the only memory and grounding on
fifteen percent of rows. Bars pinned: the prune must be free, the
growth must beat the continued control by a point on wild or half a
point on synthetic, and the grown roads must cost more to sever than
the organs they grew from.

The collider's first rung: three readings of the wild holdout, the
champion, the champion with its second grounding station severed,
and its sibling from another seed. Per slot, does disagreement between
two readings mark the slots the champion gets wrong? Bars: the front
must cover forty percent of the wrong slots with three times the base
error rate behind it. Below that the collider dies a second time, at
the slot level this time, and goes to live in the certifier.

## The order

1. **Judge.** Both results against their bars, in the ledger, before
   anything else is built. Bars do not bend.

2. **Cut the mask-prep pass down.** It spends seventy-eight minutes
   before every run computing a slot mask the machine does not use
   and a fact buffer it does. Drop the mask half, keep the facts, and
   route the pass through the compiled reader. This goes first because
   it takes an hour off every run that follows.

3. **The collider, rungs two and three,** only if rung one passes.
   Two: re-read the front's slots on the union of both readings'
   evidence and vote three ways, zero training. Three: the token gate
   born aimed at the front, under a cooker that seals the residual so
   robustness is not an escape. Its bar is the five points the
   uniform-born gate missed.

4. **The release valve.** The solver's refusal comes back into the
   loop and melts deposits in the garage, since the state has nothing
   to melt. Before a line of it is written, the headroom read: what
   does the reading do today on the rows the solver refuses? If
   refusal is invisible to the reading, that is the number to convert.

5. **The cold leg as deployment.** The sealed channel becomes the
   regime the machine runs in. A battery, a verdict script, and the
   manifest written in the same act as the word.

That is the list. Five items, two gates tonight, and a machine that
has a cycle drawn on paper and most of a cycle built in metal.
