# The stellarator: snipping the residual, and pinging the solver every breath

In August we registered a principle we called the traffic circle.
Structural mandatory routing, no bypass. The parts of the machine that
worked, the waist, the mouth, the answer key, worked because nothing
could go around them. The parts that were declined, and there were
several, were declined because something could. We built the
principle into the breath loop as a cut in the state's own path,
tried two forms, and one of them scored zero on three hundred
problems. The autopsy said it died of payload, not principle: what
crossed the cut was the average of all the slots, a global blur. We
salvaged the per-slot notebook from the wreck, closed the file, and
moved on.

This week the principle came back from three directions at once, and
this morning we re-fired it. Here is what it is, why now, and what
the first number said.

## The residual is a bypass

Our machine reads a problem seven times and revises a bank of slots
between readings. From one breath to the next, the state is carried
forward by a residual connection: the new state is the old state plus
an update. That is the standard transformer move, and it is also a
highway that every organ in the loop can be routed around. The
notebook writes ink each breath and reads it back; the garage holds
deposits; the solver hands back facts. None of them has to carry
anything, because the state carries itself.

The severance ladder this week put numbers on that. The notebook,
severed, costs four points on wild and sixteen on synthetic. The
garage costs about one point. The second slot mixer, injecting at
seventy percent of the state's band, costs nothing. Loud and
redundant, because the highway was open.

Then the two cookers taught us the law. Seal an organ's bypass on a
share of rows and the loss does not learn to use the organ. It learns
to survive the seal through the residual, which the seal left
standing. The pressure cooker was the exception, and the reason it
worked is that it severed the residual too: the committed channel, or
nothing. A cooker converts headroom only when the residual has no way
to carry the sealed rows itself.

The stellarator is that law built into geometry, on every row, with
no cooker.

## The snip

Across the six loop breaths the residual's weight runs 0.93, 0.75,
0.50, 0.25, 0.07, and then zero. The notebook's read takes the
complement. The handoff is helical and gentle, no cliff at any breath,
which is why we named the form after the fusion device whose twist is
built into its geometry rather than induced by a current. By the last
breath, nothing of the previous state crosses directly. The state the
final reading operates on is the notebook's ink, addressed by what the
state wanted to recall.

Two details matter. August's schedule stopped at five percent, because
the loop's last breath is index six of seven. Under this week's law
five percent is still a bypass, so the new schedule reaches exactly
zero. And the clock block, the sixty-four rotating planes that give
each breath its bearing, is exempt from the cut. The clock is a
coordinate system, not a road. The ink would overwrite the compass.

A traffic light is a gain: a learned scalar that says how much of an
organ gets through, which training turns down until the organ is
silent. The mask head's gain went from 0.02 to 0.0002. A roundabout is
geometry, and traffic yields to what is already in the circle. In
gradient terms, cutting the residual leaves the cross-breath gradient
exactly one path, through the ink, so the memory organ receives it by
construction rather than by invitation.

## The birth read

Before the arm trained, we read the pruned candidate with the residual
snipped at read time, no retraining. On wild it scored 0.0010. On
synthetic, 0.0000. That is the residual's removal cost on a model
trained to lean on it: everything. The notebook that carries four
points beside an open highway carries nothing when asked to be the
road, because it was never made to be. Our on-record prediction was
wild under 0.10 and synthetic under 0.50. It held and understated it.

So the arm training now starts from a floor of zero and must convert
twenty-five points of wild and ninety-seven of synthetic into the
junction. The bars are pinned: within a point of the candidate on wild
and half a point on synthetic, the notebook's removal cost up by two
points on wild or three on synthetic because it is now the road, the
clock probe intact because the exemption is a claim to be tested, and
the state's radius per breath predicted to stop climbing, because the
junction is a sink. The kill is three points below the candidate on
wild, which would reopen the August grave.

## Every breath, and what comes back

The other half of the assembly is older still. In early September we
factored the breath loop so that each breath is a separately
dispatched step with a solver call between every one. Commit the
tentative parse, run the symbolic propagation, hand back what it
found, breathe again. We proved it against the fused loop on the real
champion, forward and backward, and then set it aside.

What comes back from the solver, on every breath, is three things.
Facts: the variables it can already determine. Refusal: the parse
cannot be satisfied. And, as of this morning, the core: on a refusal,
the smallest set of factors that cannot coexist, a proof about which
slots are wrong together. We measured that signal last night on the
pruned candidate's final parses. The solver refuses half of all wrong
parses and not one of 237 right ones, and three of every four slots
the core names are actually wrong. The collider's front, by contrast,
marked wrong slots at 78% and right slots at 72%. A rumor everywhere;
a proof on half the rows.

The same build that proved the per-breath loop also recorded why the
ping had seemed to do nothing: the facts were injected into a state
that only the readout heads read. The loop never saw them. The
solver was talking to the mouth, not the hands. So the return signal
has two ports from here. Facts and the core enter the loop's own
lanes, through the mask that the next breath reads under, opening
attention among the core's slots and re-focusing their token reads on
the sentences they came from. And once the notebook is proven as the
junction, the garage's deposits join it, so that what the solver has
committed becomes part of what the state must pass through.

One highway, one roundabout. The notebook alone tonight. Then the
garage. The core comes in through the wheel, not the carry.

## What we are watching for

The state turns over by half every breath in the current family and
never freezes; the notebook is its only real memory. Under the snip,
the last breath's state is almost entirely the junction read, and the
breath gate, a learned scalar on the whole update, has closed to two
percent by then. If the radius read shows the late breaths freezing
onto the shelf, the next delta is the gate, not the junction. The
arm lands early this afternoon.
