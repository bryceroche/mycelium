# The blog opens

The Shape of Thought now has a blog. The paper on this site froze a
snapshot of the Mycelium project; this space is for what the ledger
captures day to day — registered predictions, honest negatives, and
the occasional result worth telling as a story.

A taste of the current campaign: the project recently taught its
certification machinery to grade 1,570 wild math problems against
independent answer keys. The alarm bucket — cases where the parser
confidently passed a wrong graph — came back **zero**. The same run
confirmed the parser cannot yet *read* most wild prose (it was raised
on synthetic templates and a narrower dialect), so the next generation
of the model is now training from scratch on a diet that includes the
wild text it failed on. Understanding must be learned; verification is
built in. That division of labor is the whole thesis.
