## 2. Composability: the composition matrix + the silhouette against the book of primes

The claim MATH-500 rides on: a gate trained on A and B handles A∘B. What
the record already says, sorted by grain:
- **In-register compositional DEPTH composes** — the diet's d2 cells
  (mul→add→fdiv chains) sat at 8/8 BEFORE the diet; depth was never the
  hole, the missing template was.
- **Wild-register composition fails** — the four lies are compositions in
  an unlearned register.
- **Cross-construction composition is UNMEASURED** — the open cell.

**The minimal probe: the COMPOSITION MATRIX.** For each ordered pair (A,B)
over {rel-add, rel-mul, fdiv, mod, pct, sel}, mint N solver-verified rows
where B consumes A's result. Split cells by whether the pair CO-OCCURRED in
training corpora (mechanically checkable from the mixes) or is NOVEL.
Pinned discriminator: co-occurred cells pass at baseline; the NOVEL cells
answer the question — pass = composition transfers (the gate learned an
algebra, not a phrasebook); fail = the road needs composed rows as a
corpus axis, and L4/L5 expansion needs a composition dimension. Mintable,
solver-verified, zero training.

**The silhouette's role — segment and classify against the book of
primes.** For matrix failures, the waist trajectory gives the WHERE: does
the silhouette show A's kind-signature then lose B's? Kind centroids exist
(re-anchored gen-22, all nine kinds). Segments classify against them; a
composition failure localizes to the segment whose signature degrades.
This is the silhouette-triage spec's first concrete customer — the
spec-first ruling gets its spec written around a real use.

**THE CONVERGENCE (the night's sharpest observation):** the graph-register
died as EXACT-MATCH on digests; the surviving candidate was SOFT DISTANCE.
Soft distance in silhouette-trajectory space against the certified library

## 3. The exchange family's candidate unification

Gut #109-to-be: "one universal law about how systems trade surface area
for speed and throughput." Candidate sentence:

> **A system buys throughput by collapsing interfaces and buys adaptation
> by multiplying them — and the exchange rate is set by interface
> INDEPENDENCE: correlated surface is throughput-priced but
> adaptation-worthless.**

The campaign's measured instances, read through it:
- The five-view vote: 5× surface for 1/5 throughput — surface bought
  certainty, until [1293] showed the five interfaces were CORRELATED
  (one landscape) — correlated surface, adaptation-worthless: the law's
  second clause is the [1293] finding restated.
- **The two-lever law IS the trade measured**: books = surface (every row
  hand-contacted with the wild register) and they buy TERRITORY (zone
  migration); mint = throughput (template contact only) and it buys DEPTH
  (in-zone deepening). Three datapoints already banked.
- The mouth: one cheap interface at the front buys the whole chain's
  throughput. The trace layer: near-free surface added at the margin.
- Pooling (#106): variance reduction proportional to independence — the
  same exchange-rate clause from the other side.

**The test of whether the law is real rather than decorative** (the honest
fork the countersign named): does it make a PROSPECTIVE call the
instruments don't already make? One candidate call, non-trivial: **harvest
expansion should be SURFACE-FIRST (books, hand contact) exactly at the
frontier and THROUGHPUT-FIRST (bulk automation) exactly in-register** —
bulk the known territory, hand-touch the new. That is a real decision rule
for the L4/L5 wall, and it is not something any single instrument states.
If the interps converge here, the seven seeds become one entry; if they
don't, the family's yield was its instruments and the sentence retires
honestly.
