## The three design requirements (each with its provenance)

**R1 — PER-SLOT READINESS** (#95's driver, promoted to front-runner at
the sitting; the rarest ordering: its predictions were measured before
the mechanism was proposed). A readiness state per factor slot:
constraint arrivals WET (pointer evidence, cross-sentence corroboration,
solver-relevant anchors); recency/attention-drift arrivals do NOT.
Wetted slots absorb deduction readily; dry slots resist rewriting —
and dry slots that never fill are the measured under-parse.

**R2 — REVERSIBLE COMMITMENTS** (#111's no-reverse-gear; third arrival
beside §2-R's pawl gate and the erosion finding — soft to deduction,
hard to distraction). Each binding maneuver ends at a COMMIT POINT with
explicit re-anchoring before the next; a commitment is UNDOABLE by
later constraint evidence (the reverse gear), never by mere recency
(else the parser's erosion disease is reinvented at slot grain).
The invariance verdict is the measurement of the missing reverse.

**R3 — THE OBLIQUE CUT TURNED INWARD** (#103's residue, resolved by
four nulls). No external reader grades the frontier (graph register
dead; residual/quorum/invariance/interior null). The untried axis is
the binder's OWN state: readiness distributions, revision provenance,
commit traces. The bench's instruments read from inside.

## The front-runner mechanism (sketch grade — eager-math first)

Per-slot readiness scalar r_j ∈ [0,1], parse-side only (schema
recognition is parse-side; the graph stays frame-free — the binding
theorem is constitutional):
- r_j rises on CONSTRAINT-PROVENANCE revisions (new pointer evidence,
  agreement across sentence positions, argument-role corroboration).
- r_j does NOT rise on recency/attention drift (the wetting
  distinction; #94's engagement point stated, not assumed).
- Binding COMMIT gates on r_j crossing threshold — the pawl's driver
  (form binary and measured; driver fenced, per the §2-R estate).
- REVERSE: a contradicting constraint arrival decays r_j below
  threshold and reopens the slot (undo by evidence, never by noise).
- RE-ANCHOR: at each commit point the slot's pointer evidence re-reads
  before the next maneuver begins (#111's stop-between-maneuvers).
